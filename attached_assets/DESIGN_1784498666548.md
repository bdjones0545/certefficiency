## Brand & Style

The design system is engineered to evoke a sense of focused intelligence and premium craftsmanship. It draws heavily from the Apple aesthetic—prioritizing clarity, deference to content, and a "native" feel that suggests the tool is an integral part of the user's OS. 

The personality is calm and authoritative. By utilizing generous whitespace and a "less but better" approach, the UI reduces cognitive load, allowing students to focus entirely on the learning material. The visual style is a blend of **Minimalism** and **Modern Corporate**, utilizing subtle depth and high-precision typography to create a sense of trust and longevity.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in a professional, "intelligent" spectrum. 

- **Primary:** A deep, concentrated blue for light mode and a more vibrant, backlit blue for dark mode to ensure accessibility and "glow" on OLED screens.
- **Neutrals:** We avoid pure black in light mode to prevent visual vibration, opting for a softer near-black (#1D1D1F). In dark mode, the surface tiers use subtle shifts in charcoal to define hierarchy.
- **Success:** An emerald green used sparingly for progress completion and correct answers, maintaining a restrained and sophisticated tone.
- **Surfaces:** Cards and elevated containers use pure white in light mode to pop against the warm off-white background, creating a distinct "layered" effect.

## Typography

The system utilizes **Inter** for its systematic, utilitarian, and high-legibility characteristics, which closely mirror the functional elegance of SF Pro. 

- **Hierarchy:** Display styles use tighter letter spacing and heavier weights to create a strong visual anchor. 
- **Readability:** Body text is set at 17px (the iOS standard) for optimal reading comfort during long study sessions.
- **Labels:** Small labels use increased letter spacing and semi-bold weights to maintain legibility at micro-scales.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system employs a **fixed-fluid hybrid grid**. Content is contained within a max-width of 1200px for desktop, while mobile layouts utilize a 4-column fluid structure with 20px side margins.

- **The 4px Rule:** All spacing increments must be multiples of 4px to ensure mathematical harmony.
- **Whitespace:** Use generous vertical padding between sections (stack-lg) to allow the UI to "breathe."
- **Alignment:** Consistent left-alignment is preferred for all educational content to aid scanning. Horizontal scrolling is reserved for secondary actions or "Quick Card" carousels to save vertical space.

## Elevation & Depth

Hierarchy is established through **Tonal Layering** rather than heavy shadows.

- **Level 0 (Background):** #F9F9F9. The canvas.
- **Level 1 (Cards/Sheets):** Pure white with a 1px border (#E5E5E7). This is the primary surface for content.
- **Level 2 (Modals/Popovers):** Pure white with a very soft, diffused shadow (0px 10px 30px rgba(0,0,0,0.05)).
- **Glassmorphism:** Navigation bars and bottom-docked composers should use a backdrop blur (20px) with 80% opacity to maintain context of the content scrolling beneath them.

## Shapes

The shape language is defined by large, "squircle"-inspired radii. 

- **Primary Radius:** 20px for main cards and containers.
- **Component Radius:** 12px for buttons and input fields.
- **Interactive Elements:** Chat bubbles use a 20px radius, with a smaller 4px radius on the corner adjacent to the user/AI avatar to indicate directionality.

## Components

- **Chat Bubbles:** AI bubbles are surface-colored (white) with a subtle border. User bubbles are Primary Blue with white text. Both use 20px corner rounding.
- **Message Composer:** A bottom-docked, floating bar. It should feature a "glass" background blur, a pill-shaped text input, and a circular "Send" button that only turns Primary Blue when text is present.
- **Quick-Action Cards:** Small, horizontally-scrolling cards (approx 160px wide) with a 20px radius. They contain an icon and a brief label, used for suggested study topics.
- **Progress Indicators:** Use thin (4px) linear bars. The background track should be a very light gray (#E5E5E7), with the progress fill in Restrained Emerald.
- **Buttons:** Large, pill-shaped (height 48px). Primary buttons use a solid fill; secondary buttons use a 1px border with no fill.
- **Input Fields:** 12px rounded corners, 1px light border, with labels placed above the field in the `label-caps` style.
