# CertEfficiency Premium Video Course Implementation

## Objective

Create a premium video course section inside CertEfficiency that allows users to watch the free introduction video before requiring payment to unlock the remaining lessons.

The purchase should be powered by our existing Stripe account under **Efficiency Strength Training**.

# Technical Requirements

* Embed HeyGen-hosted videos securely.
* Do not expose premium lesson URLs before purchase.
* Verify access server-side before returning premium video metadata.
* Use Stripe webhooks to grant course access after successful payment rather than relying on client-side redirects.
* Persist progress so users can resume across devices.
* Prepare the architecture so additional courses can be added later without redesigning the system.

The implementation should be production-ready, secure, scalable, and polished enough to serve as the foundation for all future CertEfficiency premium courses.
